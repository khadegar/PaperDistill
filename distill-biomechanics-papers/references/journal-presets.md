## 1. Governing rule

Use journal presets to improve coverage and venue matching, never as a default whitelist for literature inclusion.

- Default discovery mode: `broad_relevant`
- Optional mode: `core_plus_specialist`
- User-requested restrictive mode: `top_journal_focus`

In `broad_relevant`, include any relevant, traceable scholarly item that meets the study-level criteria. Label preprints, proceedings, theses, standards, and non-peer-reviewed materials explicitly. Evaluate the paper itself before weighting its findings.

## 3. Expanded discovery strata

### A. Biomechanics and computational mechanics

Always search across relevant titles including:

- Journal of Biomechanics
- Biomechanics and Modeling in Mechanobiology
- Journal of Biomechanical Engineering
- Computer Methods in Biomechanics and Biomedical Engineering
- Medical Engineering & Physics
- Clinical Biomechanics
- Annals of Biomedical Engineering
- Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing
- Journal of The Royal Society Interface
- International Journal for Numerical Methods in Biomedical Engineering
- Computers in Biology and Medicine
- Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine

For method development, also include CMAME, Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization, International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering, Computers & Structures, International Journal of Mechanical Sciences, Engineering Structures, Journal of Applied Mechanics, and related mechanics outlets.

### B. Orthopaedics, bone, spine, and implants

Include field-leading and specialist/clinical sources:

- Journal of Orthopaedic Research
- Bone
- Journal of Bone and Mineral Research
- Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery
- Journal of Orthopaedic Translation
- Journal of Orthopaedic Surgery and Research
- Injury
- The Spine Journal
- Spine
- European Spine Journal
- World Neurosurgery
- The Journal of Arthroplasty
- International Orthopaedics
- Archives of Orthopaedic and Trauma Surgery
- BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders
- Osteoporosis International
- Calcified Tissue International
- Bone Reports

Do not discard a clinically relevant cohort or device study because it appears in an application journal rather than a mechanics journal.

### C. Biomaterials and porous scaffolds

Include:

- Biomaterials
- Acta Biomaterialia
- Bioactive Materials
- Journal of the Mechanical Behavior of Biomedical Materials
- Journal of Biomedical Materials Research Part A

- Regenerative Biomaterials
- Biomaterials Science
- Biomedical Materials
- Journal of Materials Science: Materials in Medicine
- Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology
- Biofabrication

Also search broad materials journals when the paper contains transferable process-structure-property evidence.

### D. Topology optimization and design

Include:

- Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization
- Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering
- Materials & Design
- Computer-Aided Design
- Journal of Mechanical Design
- Advances in Engineering Software

Require a clear biomedical bridge before using a purely structural paper as biological evidence; it may still provide a method, constraint, or validation pattern.

### E. Additive manufacturing and fatigue

Include:

- Additive Manufacturing

- Metals, Materials, Polymers, and Applied Sciences when directly relevant

Broad multidisciplinary journals may contribute evidence, but require the same study-level appraisal as specialist outlets.

## 4. Normalization rules

Before frequency analysis or journal matching:

- Normalize case and whitespace.
- Convert `Ieee` to `IEEE` and normalize ampersands.
- Merge standard abbreviations with full journal names when unambiguous.
- Merge `Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers, Part H` with its full subtitle form when they denote the same source.
- Normalize `Scientific reports` to `Scientific Reports`.
- Track former/successor titles separately and link them; do not rewrite historical citations to a current title.
- Do not merge journals solely because their names are similar.

## 5. Venue recommendation

Use a current 100-point fit score:

| Dimension | Points |
|---|---:|
| Scope and readership fit | 30 |
| Evidence and methodological maturity | 25 |
| Novelty and contribution fit | 15 |
| Accepted article type | 10 |
| Current field position/metrics | 10 |
| APC, timing, format, and practical constraints | 10 |

Classify:

- `aspirational`: 80-100 with no hard mismatch
- `target`: 65-79
- `backup`: 50-64
- `exclude`: hard scope/article-type mismatch or score below 50

Verify official scope and current author instructions at recommendation time. Important current examples include journals that do not accept review papers or that accept only particular review formats. Record `checked_at`, official URL, metrics year, and any hard gate.

Keep the venue decision separate from corpus inclusion: a paper from a backup or long-tail journal may still contain the most direct or reproducible evidence for a claim.
